## Content

### Research Results

Testing results of WhaleRapy show:
- Improved emotional awareness: users better understood their emotional states through AI facial emotion analysis.
- Effective personalisation: emotion recognition combined with scent preference surveys generated aroma formulas users found relevant and enjoyable.
- High user satisfaction: users rated convenience, uniqueness, and relaxing effects highly.

### Product Highlights

- **AI Emotion Recognition:** analyses facial expressions and personalises aromatherapy based on emotional state.
- **Personalised Aroma Formula:** combines emotion analysis and scent preferences for a unique fragrance blend.
- **Automated and easy to use:** dispenses customised scents with minimal user interaction.
- **Relaxing and accessible:** suitable for homes, schools, offices, and anyone seeking emotional balance.

### Target Audience

Students under stress, office workers, wellness enthusiasts, homes and schools, anyone seeking relaxation.

### Why Choose Whalerapy?

Millions of people experience stress, anxiety, and emotional imbalance. Traditional aromatherapy provides the same scent for everyone. Whalerapy uses AI facial emotion recognition and personalised scent preferences to create a customised aroma formula per user.
