# City Savior

City Savior is an assistive technology project built around a smart wheelchair and a heart rate monitoring system. It was designed in the context of smart city applications.

## Components

- **Wheelchair control:** `Weelchair.py` / `wheelchair.py` handle movement logic.
- **Heart rate monitoring:** `heartrate_monitor.py`, `hrcalc.py`, and `max30102.py` interface with the MAX30102 pulse oximeter sensor to track the user's heart rate in real time.
- **Smart walker:** A related sub-component documented in `smart walker.pdf`.

## Technologies

- Python.
- MAX30102 pulse oximeter and heart rate sensor.
