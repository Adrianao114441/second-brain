# Yves Wong

Yves Wong is a team member on the [[Whalerapy]] Stanford competition submission, credited alongside Adrian Ao and [[Gabriel Lo]] on the WhaleRapy Stanford Poster (60 × 160 cm, 2026-07-07).

## Known Collaboration

- **[[Whalerapy]] (Stanford track):** co-author of the Stanford competition submission. The team is Adrian Ao, Yves Wong, and [[Gabriel Lo]], all from Pui Ching Middle School (Coloane Campus).

## Notes

Yves Wong appears only in the Stanford submission materials. The WRO / brochure materials credit a different team (Oscar Ao, Cabrina Wong, Abigail Choi).
