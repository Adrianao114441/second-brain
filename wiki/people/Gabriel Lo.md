# Gabriel Lo

Gabriel Lo is a team member on the [[Whalerapy]] Stanford competition submission, credited alongside Adrian Ao and Yves Wong on the WhaleRapy Stanford Poster (60 × 160 cm, 2026-07-07).

## Known Collaboration

- **[[Whalerapy]] (Stanford track):** co-author of the Stanford competition submission. The team is Adrian Ao, Gabriel Lo, and Yves Wong, all from Pui Ching Middle School (Coloane Campus).

## Notes

Gabriel Lo appears only in the Stanford submission materials. The WRO / brochure materials credit a different team (Oscar Ao, Cabrina Wong, Abigail Choi), suggesting the two submissions may have had separate author credits or team compositions.
