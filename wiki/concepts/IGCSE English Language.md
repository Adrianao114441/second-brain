# IGCSE English Language

The Edexcel IGCSE English Language A examination tests reading comprehension, language analysis, and writing skills across two papers.

## Paper 1, Question 4

Question 4 (12 marks) appears in Paper 1 Section A. It tests **AO2**: understanding and analysing how writers use linguistic and structural devices.

It is always based on one of the 10 texts in the Pearson Edexcel IGCSE English Anthology (non-fiction, Part 1).

### Question Types

- **Type A:** "How does the writer use language and structure to interest and engage the reader?"
- **Type B:** "How does the writer use language and structure to show the writer's thoughts and feelings?"
- **Type C:** "How does the writer use language and structure to describe their experiences?" (less common)

### The 10 Anthology Texts

| Text | Author | Common Q4 Focus |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| A Passage to Africa | George Alagiah | Interest and engage |
| The Explorer's Daughter | Kari Herbert | Interest and engage / thoughts and feelings |
| H is for Hawk | Helen Macdonald | Thoughts and feelings |
| Beyond the Sky and the Earth | Jamie Zeppa | Thoughts and feelings |
| Explorers or Boys Messing About? | Tom Morris | Convey opinion |
| Between a Rock and a Hard Place | Aron Ralston | Convey difficulties |
| A Game of Polo with a Headless Goat | Emma Levine | Interest and engage |
| Chinese Cinderella | Adeline Yen Mah | Thoughts and feelings |
| The Danger of a Single Story | Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie | Convey thoughts and perspectives |
| Young and Dyslexic? You've Got It Made | Benjamin Zephaniah | Convey personal experience |

### Key Analytical Techniques to Discuss

- Imagery and sensory language.
- Structural choices (chronological order, narrative focus, pacing).
- Tone shifts and juxtaposition.
- Rhetorical devices (rhetorical questions, direct address, repetition).
- Sentence length variation for effect.
- First-person narration and personal voice.

### High-Frequency Paper Coverage (Q4 Appearances)

- A Passage to Africa — 5 appearances.
- H is for Hawk — 3 appearances.
- The Explorer's Daughter — 4 appearances.
- A Game of Polo with a Headless Goat — 3 appearances.
